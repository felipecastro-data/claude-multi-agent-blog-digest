## Claude Tag now supports personal connectors in channels
- Claude Tag (Slack integration) now lets users bring their own personal connectors (Google Drive, calendar, CRM) into channel requests
- Previously limited to admin-provisioned, shared channel connectors only
- New "review mode" (approve before posting) and "auto mode" (auto-post unless sensitive content detected)
- Enterprise admins get added governance/logging controls tied to personal accounts
- Respects existing role-based access controls; enables supervised work needing individual data sources
